    A Lean 4 Formalisation of OpenAI’s Proof of the Cycle Double Cover Conjecture

    Vaibhav Bajpai, Johannes Carmesin, Utku Okur

    Lean code: GitHub, doi:10.5281/zenodo.21410822.

    See also J. Carmesin, 10 Scalable Challenges in Combinatorics: A Public Benchmark Formalised in Lean.

    Preprint ↗ Lean code ↗ Zenodo ↗ 
    theorem CDC_multi {W : Type*} {E : Type*}
  [DecidableEq W] [Finite W] [Fintype E]
    {G : Multigraph W E} (hb : G.Bridgeless) :
    ∃ (n : ℕ) (C : Fin n → E → ZMod 2), G.IsCycleDoubleCover C := by
  classical
  letI : Fintype W := Fintype.ofFinite W
  refine G.exists_isCycleDoubleCover_of_forall_cubic ?_ hb
  intro W' _ _ G' _ hcubic hbG
  obtain ⟨n, C, hC⟩ := G'.exists_cycleDoubleCover hcubic hbG
  exact ⟨n, C, G'.isCycleDoubleCover_toMultigraph hC⟩

    The final theorem of the formalisation, for every finite bridgeless multigraph (CDC/CDC_multigraph/Main_multi.lean). It depends only on Lean’s standard axioms.
