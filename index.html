Research interests

Combinatorics, formal proof and AI

My projects sit where these three areas meet: stating combinatorial results precisely in Lean 4, proving them, and building AI systems whose output the Lean kernel can check.

Combinatorics

Graph theory and combinatorics, from the Cycle Double Cover Conjecture to challenge problems that scale in difficulty.

Lean 4

Formalising combinatorial proofs in Lean 4 and Mathlib, so that every step is checked by the Lean kernel.

AI

Language models combined with proof search and verification, aimed at automated reasoning that can be audited.
